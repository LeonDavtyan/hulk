# Golden Hulk: Skybound

A cheerful browser platform game. Golden Hulk has red hair, jumps between clouds, collects suns, grows with mushrooms, and becomes strong enough to bump storm clouds away after collecting a star.

**Play online:** https://leondavtyan.github.io/hulk/

Open `index.html` in a browser to play locally. No install or build step is needed.

**Controls:** Left/Right arrows or A/D to move; Space, Up, or W to jump; R to restart. Touch buttons appear on phones and tablets. Collect at least 12 of the 24 suns, then reach the rainbow gate.
